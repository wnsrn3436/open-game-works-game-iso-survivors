## style

Stylized low-poly 3D with hand-painted textures. Chunky, readable silhouettes for the isometric camera.

## palette

- `#1B1A2E` night
- `#3E5C3A` moss
- `#C9A227` lantern gold
- `#8E1F2F` blood red
- `#E8E1D0` bone

## mood

A tense night siege that turns into a power fantasy. Warm light pushing back the endless dark.
