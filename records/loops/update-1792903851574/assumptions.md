## Which UI text is drawn in the heading text style, and at which weights?
- decision: `LocalizationCore.bodyFontWeight` 400 and `LocalizationCore.headingFontWeight` 800. `HeadingButton` on every `Button` of the scenes and prefabs (the terms `CheckBox` rows stay body, since `HeadingButton` has the base type `Button` and would drop the check icons); `HeadingLabel` on titles and headers, HUD numbers (health, timer, level, kills, gold, slot levels), record and result stat values, card and upgrade names and levels, card tags, the badge count, banners and warnings. Descriptions, hints, stat names, the tagline and the user ID line stay body
- evidence: `records/core-proposals/1792800900000-font-weight.md` (`## problem`: heavy titles, buttons and HUD numbers, regular body text) and the proposal's example weights 400 and 800; `NotoSansKR[wght]` (`font/main`, the only font of both languages) has a `wght` axis of 100 to 900, and the instance log had no warn or error line. The labels picked for heading are those the workaround drew heaviest (outline about 1/11 to 1/13 of the font size)

## Where does the `titles_and_buttons` save fixture leave the instance?
- decision: one `lobby.open_lobby` setup step on a new account; after the runner's restart the client boots through the title screen to the camp with 0 gold, so the shrine shows disabled-looking buy buttons and the first run's first level-up comes at about 0:07
- evidence: `tools/src/commands/instance/lib/supervisor.ts` `runFixture` restarts the client after the steps, so no fixture can end on the title screen; the title scene advances on its own after login. Checked on an instance: camp, `title.open_title`, shrine, level-up, pause and give-up result all drew heading text at weight 800
